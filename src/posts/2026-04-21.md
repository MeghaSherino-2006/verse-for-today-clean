### Verse for Today

> ‘Our fathers ate the manna in the wilderness; as it is written, He gave them bread from Heaven to eat’ 
> **[John 6:31]**

---

### Reflection

Forty years in the wilderness, God provided for His people, those whom He delivered from the slavery of Egypt to bring them to the promised land. This provision sustained their lives to withstand the odds and oddities of the desert. It also prepared them to know God, trust in Him, and give glory to Him as His favoured people. They were given the Laws – the commandments by God to be observed, all for moulding them to be partakers in God’s plan for saving mankind. They were made successful in possessing the land promised to them, not because of their righteousness, but because it was within the Divine plan for them. 

This ‘reigning’ down of Manna, the bread from Heaven, was a prefiguration of a similar event to unravel in their lives in due course of time, with the Messiah’s coming. And when Jesus incarnated as ‘man’, He reminded them of the food that endures to Eternal life. This was soon after they were provided bread (the miracle of the five bread and two fishes distributed to the five thousand). Though Jesus told them that He was the bread of life, the manna from Heaven to be given to all that they may live, their hearts were incapable of perceiving that the Hands of God were powerful to do it. Yet in all their unworthiness and doubt, they pleaded with Him – Sir, give us this bread always. 

It does not matter whether we truly understand the mystery of God becoming bread for us, but what matters is a humble heart (in us) to approach Him and ask Him to give us this bread every day. Jesus understands our weaknesses, our inadequacies, the constraints in us that inhibit us from imbibing His Word in its fullness, therefore, what He expects from us is merely this willingness to love Him, to trust Him, and receive from Him with a yielding spirit. Our forefathers trusted God, received His favour and providence. This is more than a precedent for us to emulate them in giving honor to the Lord in the Eucharist and thanking Him for everything each day. Amen
