### Verse for Today

> ‘He said, But I will be with you, and this shall be the sign for you, that I have sent you; when you have brought the people out of Egypt, you shall serve God on this mountain’  
> **[Exodus 3:12]**

---

### Reflection

Every worship is a remembrance, a sign of the myriad ways we have been redeemed and reclaimed by the Lord. To the Israelites, even before they were brought out to the ‘promised’ land, God spoke to them of their worshipping Him at Mount Horeb. Moses was reminded of His call to lead the Israelites out from Egypt to the land where God had chosen for them, while he stood at the foot of this mountain. For us too, who prepare to worship the Lord this Sunday, the Risen Lord appears with His manifold gifts and graces. 

The gifts and graces are the spoils of the great victory He won for us on the Cross, all that had been confiscated from us by the evil one. Jesus comes to restore, revive, and complete our ascension to the Heavens with Him. St Paul explains this further for us by making known to us, that we were dead in sins and trespasses, slaves to the ruler of the world. But God in His bounty of ‘mercy and compassion for us’ destined that we (whom He loved so much) must not only be saved but also be seated with Jesus in the Heavenly places. Every one of us has fallen from this preternatural grace given to us in the beginning of creation. Now what we choose makes all the difference – whether we choose life, by looking at the Lord, or slavery by continuing our old ways. The Holy Spirit leads us to fathom the depths of gifts and grace that are open to us (the spoils of victory of Jesus) in every Eucharistic celebration. 

God calls us today to Himself to exercise our authority as sons of the Kingdom, to a great sense of victory and renown, the power of resurrection. What the Early Church partook of, what empowered the apostles to go to the ends of the world with Christ’s message, is our call too. 

May you be blessed to obtain His glory. Amen
