### Verse for Today

> ‘As Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, so must the Son of Man be lifted up’   
> **[John 3:14]**

---

### Reflection

What was within the hearts of the people, their ingratitude, anger, and resentment towards God, which was voiced out to Moses as threats, took the form of serpents. God made these venomous serpents appear before their very eyes to show them what actually caused their fall. 

Dwindling faith and trust in a loving God, owing to the hardness of their hearts to perceive, believe, surrender, and submit to God. It was false pride, a sense of overt independence to fix things and situations their own way, regardless of whether it meant derision to God and inconvenience to another person. Jesus quoted this instance from the Book of Numbers to reveal to them what brings their Salvation. If only they would admit their mistakes, iniquities of the past, and renounce their crooked ways, would real Salvation be made available to them when they called out to the Lord, for it was for their sins, curses, and frailties that the Son of Man was raised as a symbol of humiliation on the Cross. 

God wants us first to be aware of who we are. To understand the traits that build up our personality, the attitudes that clog our minds from seeing the truth. At times, there are those inhibitions from within that impede us from accepting our wrong and making amends to our ways. If so, let us offer these to the risen Lord, for Him to sanctify and amend; Just a word from Him will do for us to make a come-back.
