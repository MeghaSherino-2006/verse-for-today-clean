### Verse for Today

> ‘Perceiving then that they were about to come and take him by force to make him king, Jesus withdrew again to the mountain by himself’  
> **[John 6:15]**

---

### Reflection

Why would a forceful coronation be needed for the One who was already a King? And this is how, at times, things work out in the world we are part of. 

The world creates prototype heroes to suit its daily needs. Those who are seated in positions of authority are those most likely tempted to do so to ensure their targets and goals are easily met. Of course, interdependence is the law of any society, and there is no harm in people helping one another. But toxicity arises when these iconic heroes are manipulated or forced to play puppets in the hands of their masters. The free will, dignity, and rights of this individual is suppressed and subjected to a lesser cause, which is what proves the entire process of creation of the prototype hero as erroneous. 

When the crowds saw that Jesus provided bread (food) to large numbers miraculously, instead of giving glory to God and honoring Him as the Messiah, these were inclined to make use of his powers to overthrow the Roman rule. Without consultation or consent with Jesus, they rashly moved in to execute their plans, which were nowhere close/related to Jesus’s purpose in life. How the Lord responded to this kind of public gesture/ acclamation/appreciation? (a faint one) is what you and I have been called to direct our attention to. Not to outrightly dissent, or oppose them, or explain our stand, but to withdraw from there firstly, to retreat from the noise and seek a place of silence and calm. 

To be clear and decisive (about ourselves) is one thing, and to learn to ignore criticism, smirks, and subtle talks is another. And both these create in us ‘character’ that is strong, unyielding to threats, subtleties, and undue adulation from people around. It is the Spirit of the Lord that strengthens us with the resolve to walk our purpose, to fear none and walk our talk in humble submission to the Divine will.
