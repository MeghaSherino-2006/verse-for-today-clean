### Verse for Today

> ‘…for the ruler of this world is coming. He has no claim on me, but I do as the Father has commanded me so that the world may know that I love the Father’ 
> **[John 14:30,31]**

---

### Reflection

Jesus was almost at the point where the purpose of His life was to be fulfilled. The crucifixion was certain, and just around the corner, He knew that his disciples were about to abandon Him, one of them was to betray Him to the authorities...

All these were evil and were the works of the ruler of this world. But here Jesus clarifies that though evil took an upper hand at this hour, and it seemed to everyone as though the evil one was calling the shots, that was however, not the reality. Because it was Jesus who willed to obey His Father, on account of His love and reverence for Him, that permitted the series of events to transpire. 

Obedience has such an unimaginable power we often fail to understand (or overlook), that touches the heart of God to transform and bring in miracles in life. It may seem painful, (at times) for one to obey especially so, when he finds that his personal freedom, dignity, and image are at stake, when ‘individual interests’ are overrun by public demands, or when one side of the story is unheard of. Most of the times, the excuses for disobedience comes from one or more of the above reasons or from many others. Whatever be the situation we are placed into, whenever our Spirits submit to authority (legitimate), the Lord is with us, and the Prince of this world loses his claim over us. For a while, just like Jesus, we may have to undergo sufferings, or pain, misunderstanding, and dishonour; but in the long run, we will be triumphant, because Heaven will uphold our cause.
