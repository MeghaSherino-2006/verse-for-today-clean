### Verse for Today

> ‘But the Advocate, the Holy Spirit, whom the Father will send in my name will teach you everything and remind you of all that I have said to you’  
> **[John 14:26]**

---

### Reflection

Sometimes from nowhere comes a comfort, an assurance that all is well under the control of the Almighty, and where does this belief come from? The Spirit of the Lord in us. Even when smothered by uncertainties, lack of clarity, and direction, this Spirit, whom Jesus called ‘Our Advocate’, whispers to us ‘hope’ and shows us a way. Unawares, we sometimes express a sense of courage and wit to do certain things (decisive), we would otherwise never have done, or that which everyone must have discouraged us from trying to – this Spirit in us was our inspiration. When all human help fades, and the path ahead begins to stumble, it is this ‘Counselor’ - the Holy Spirit who prompts us to act unwaveringly in faith, promising us a more than expected favourable outcome. The love of the Lord, in its length, breadth, height, and depth is made manifest to us by this power of the Spirit who enlightens our minds and hearts. We are called to embrace a sense of belongingness to the Father above, because of the Holy Spirit in us. What this Spirit of Jesus does to us and from within us is yet not exhaustively described, because of the limits of our human thoughts, imagination, and understanding. Now, you and I would be able to understand why David, the King (after having grievously sinned) pleaded to God that the Holy Spirit may not leave him. And for us, the New Israel redeemed by the blood of Jesus Christ, ‘who’/’what’ can stand in our way, if God, the Holy Spirit is with us?
