### Verse for Today

> ' He made my feet like the feet of deer, and set me secure on the heights'  
> **[2 Samuel 22:34]**

---

### Reflection

As the summers have grown unusually hot these days, who would not prefer the heights ( higher altitudes)? 

A respite from the scorching heat, one that relieves the stress within, makes one feel comfortable and in control, more than all away from the noises from the ground far below. Now to stand on the heights' is another thing, calling for courage, prudence, and a balanced emotional state, one that is cautious of all the possible risks that could come our way. Therefore this one stands because he/ she is on firm ground, faith in the one who has brought him/ her this far and of the trust that He will do the rest. When David praised God describing His Providence by way of the metaphor of the deer's feet, he believed that he was on firm ground, though surrounded by enemies, he was safe as he was within the protective Hands of the Lord, and all the strength needed to make the next move for survival would be opened up by God. 

Let David's prayer be ours too-

---

### Prayer

We pray for grace and mercy to surround us, hide us and reclaim us from all that poses an adversity for us. The Lord who brought us this far, will lead us further ahead. May His name be glorified. Amen
