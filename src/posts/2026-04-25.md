### Verse for Today

> ‘Shepherd the flock of God that is among you, exercising  oversight, not under compulsion, but willingness, as God would have you, not for shameful gain, but eagerly, not domineering over those in your charge but being examples to the flock’  
> **[1Peter 5:2-3]**

---

### Reflection

Shepherding is essentially a demanding endeavour, as the one who shepherds takes charge of the flock of God. 

This need not necessarily be in the sense of the Church and people in their care, but it revolves around every kind of relationship where one has an influence over another. It can relate to parents in a family, spouses among themselves, members in a community, the talents we harbour, our skills and niche qualities…..In all these instances, the one who owns possession also owns responsibility to help grow, contribute to the betterment of society, to guide and equip to its fullest potential, leaving behind personal or individual self-interests, and with a sense of commitment that pleases God. 

Jesus set before us an example of His, as a good shepherd, who came to give life in abundance to his flock. How He led His flock is the crux for us to emulate in every walk of life. He never expressed the know-it-all attitude, never despised or condemned the ignorant, never subdued the weak or the helpless, but always strengthened them with love and encouragement that could go all the way in building them up in the long run. Not that he never pointed to their faults and corrected them. He did so, and even promised them a helper/a Paraclete who would assist them in His absence. 

Let us examine what motivates us as shepherds in our own domains: Is it the position, dominance, and power of superiority? That the final word would always be ours? Of the crowds before whom we may seem impressive, of successes, of personal ambition and renown? Or is it that by our deeds and words, the flock of God is fattened, satisfied and served? 
Let us ask the Lord to show us where our ways and attitudes need change. Amen
