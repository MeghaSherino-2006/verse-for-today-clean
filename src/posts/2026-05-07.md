### Verse for Today

> ‘But we believe that we will be saved through the grace of the Lord Jesus, just as they will’ 
> **[Acts 15:11]**

---

### Reflection

It was at a time when a great rift/divide was about to occur in the Early Church. The controversy was the need for cultural affinity within the Church between Jewish believers and Gentiles. However, what erupted as a major deterrent to the growth of the fledgling ‘Early Church’ was quieted by the grit and courage demonstrated by Peter’s (timely) decision to go in for inclusiveness. The doors of Mother Church were opened wide for all who believed in Jesus Christ, irrespective of religious outlook, cultural inclinations, or societal stature. Therefore, these (Christians) were called ‘followers of the way’ as, to the world, their lifestyle/pattern of living was distinct and aligned to Jesus who was the way, the truth, and the life. The words that Peter spoke in the midst of the Council (Synod) at Jerusalem explained the dynamics of the system (of the Church of Christ), and thus proved a game- changer then. Each who believes in Jesus is saved by grace, like us. This meant that neither any of our efforts nor our desire/(will) got us to where we are today. It was merely God’s mercy and love for us that brought us to this day and state. The humility underlying this statement is what needs to ring alarm bells in our minds. At a time when, for the most trivial reasons, we have been vying with, competing with, and asserting ourselves over others in our community, Churches and families, the Spirit of the Lord speaks to us through the words of Peter, nudging us to accept the fact that it is by grace that we are saved, and not by works or deeds of ours. 

Let us humbly acknowledge this and submit ourselves to the will of God always. Amen
