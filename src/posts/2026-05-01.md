### Verse for Today

> ‘and coming to his hometown, he taught them in their synagogue, so that they were astonished and said, “Where did this man get this wisdom and these mighty works?”’  
> **[Matthew 13:54]**

---

### Reflection

It was prejudice that dominated their intellect and reasoning that the Messiah who came to them was ignored and rejected. Consequently, the grace that was offered to build them up became ineffective/ invalid to them. We read that they took offense at Jesus, rather than accepting Him the way He was. And thus, He did not perform the many mighty works that were done to those in other cities. 

If we were to connect this to our personal lives, how many instances have we encountered when we failed to acknowledge the people around us, those woven into our lives, our families, friends, neighbours, our communities, as those placed by God in our life situations. There have been people whom we have completely ignored, a few whom we have kept at a distance with (for various reasons), people from whom we have encountered bitter experiences, those who have despised us, the weak, incapable, less civilized... 

To perceive without the lens of hypocrisy, social stigma, or whatever be the predispositions in our minds that regulate our behaviour and outlook, this is where we need support and grace from the Lord. Lord, teach us to see each person (around us) as an instrument of grace, an opportunity for you to intervene in our lives. Do not permit us to despise any, perpetrate hatred among others, indulge in tittle-tattle, grapevine/gossip, and thereby create rifts in society.
