### Verse for Today

> ‘I have come into the world as light, so that whoever believes in me may not remain in darkness’   
> **[John 12:46]**

---

### Reflection

Light and darkness were the Created, but not for infinity. To them was destined a specific allotted time and space, so that life could be sustained and prolonged. Jesus declared that He came that we may have life and ‘life in abundance’, also cautioning that the thief comes only to steal, to kill, and destroy. That, however strong you and I may seem to be, we are easily susceptible to the deception of the thief, who conveniently sneaks in, clouding our way forward, bringing in doubt, hopelessness, despair, unbelief, and all that is negative. And here we are called to believe and convince ourselves that even through the darkest clouds, the Light (Jesus) finds His way. So, not to despair and grieve in the grip of darkness, which is temporal, but to seek after the Light, and be girded to move forward. 

It is this conviction of our minds, our thought processes, our determination, that coerces in us a sense of longing for the Lord in prayer. Darkness, illness, deprivations are bound to come in life, to knock down this firmness in us (the work of the Evil one), things may never seem what they meant to be in the outset to us, but we are promised that though the mountains shall depart, and hills be removed, His unfailing love will stand with us. And when swirled within the unimaginable love of the Lord, we have nothing to be worried about. Just keep telling the Lord (each moment in life) what you feel and that you need Him more. He will work for you, taking you to the finest pastures, even when you feel stuck in life’s crossroads. His Light and the joy He gives will be your strength. Amen
