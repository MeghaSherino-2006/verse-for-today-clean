### Verse for Today

> ‘Let the Word of Christ dwell in you richly, teaching and admonishing one another in all wisdom, singing psalms and hymns and spiritual songs, with thankfulness in your hearts to God’ 
> **[Colossians 3:16]**

---

### Reflection

If an Operating system does the wiring, instructions, and programming for all other parts of the computer, its Hardware and software, it is our minds that algorithmize our actions, words, deeds, expressions, and attitudes. Therefore, becoming a renewed person/better person would essentially require a rewiring in our thought processes, our perceptions, beliefs, and attitudes. 

The human mind is so intricate, so complex, yet so subtle, to be easily maneuvered by external influences. Let the double-edged sword of the Word of the Lord do the wiring in our minds each day. And this is precisely what St Paul exhorts us to do, by offering ourselves to be enriched by the Word, to be taught, admonished, filled with wisdom, by the Word. If reading the Word alone may distract us, let us accustom ourselves to singing psalms, praises, hymns of gratitude to the Lord. It is in this way we discipline ourselves, pay heed to what matters most in life, learn to forgive others, and reorient ourselves to become vessels of grace channelled to the world. In other words, the most feasible way to invite the Spirit of the Lord to transform us is by opening our hearts to God’s Word. Let each day therefore inspire us to welcome the Holy Spirit to guide us, open closed doors in life, and empower us with the strength to be like Christ in every way. Amen
