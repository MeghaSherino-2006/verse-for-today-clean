### Verse for Today

> ‘Slaves, obey your earthly masters with fear and trembling, with a sincere heart, as you would Christ, not by the way of eye-service, as people-pleasers, but as slaves of Christ, doing the will of God from the heart, rendering service with a good will as to the Lord and not to man, knowing that whatever good anyone does, he will receive back from the Lord, whether he is a slave or is free’  
> **[Ephesians 6:5-8]**

---

### Reflection

The Holy Spirit inspired Paul, the apostle, to realize that his true worth was not relative to his earthly master/masters. The same Spirit asks you and me this question - Who is your real master?

Following the footsteps of St. Paul, we are led to understand that we serve Jesus Christ and Him alone. Serving Christ would inevitably mean doing all things in alignment with the promptings of His Spirit. Jesus was never concerned about pleasing men around Him. He was totally engaged in fulfilling the will of His Father, who sent Him with the purpose of saving all men. Even when despised, condemned, and underestimated by the religious leaders and authorities who ruled during His time, Jesus was not cowed down. Nothing could deter Him from doing the tasks that were assigned to Him by His Father. His love never faded, though His Spirit grieved seeing the hypocrisy of people. All the works of mercy that were destined to be fulfilled through Him while on earth, He attended to them with utmost sincerity and goodwill. 

So are we called to be – to emulate Christ in every way, nonchalantly, looking not on the reward from human authorities, but expecting to be rewarded more than we deserve from the Lord who compensates for even the slightest of deeds of goodwill. Let this conviction be our inspiration and the game-changer of ‘who we are’ each day. Always believe: Our true master is Jesus Christ, and to Him alone will we need to give an account of/ a narrative/justification for all that describes our journey on this earth. It is the Lord who maketh poor, maketh rich; bringeth low, and lifteth up. Amen
