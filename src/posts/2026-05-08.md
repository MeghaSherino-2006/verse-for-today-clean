### Verse for Today

> ‘You did not choose me, but I chose you and appointed you that you should go and bear fruit and that your fruit should abide, so that whatever you ask the Father in my name, he will give it to you’  
> **[John 15:16]**

---

### Reflection

You are reading this Word from the Lord because He chose you. He chose you out of the world, with a distinct calling in life. What may define my purpose in life may not prove helpful to another, because each is unique and called for a distinct set of tasks to fulfil. And here rises the caveat of our calling, that there is every possibility that when you and I pursue our call, we will be hated by the world. To be disliked or rejected by the world is disheartening, but to be envied by others is even harder to bear. 

St. Paul understood these realities of the Church community, and tried to address this negativity by exhorting believers to test his/her own works. By comparing our motives, attitudes, instincts, deeds, and words, whether they attune to the truth of the Gospel, one ensures that he/she has taken up his/her purpose of life to its fullness, and not rating them in the balance (benchmark) of deeds, words, actions, or attitudes of another, or as per the diktats of the crowd. This can be practised in life ‘only’ when Jesus is our ‘master’ and when we ‘truly’ love Him. Thus, Jesus asks us to go forth and bear fruit, to be on the run, to produce fruits that last (not short-lived), those that have a ripple effect to bring life to many. It is then that whatever we may have asked for in prayer just happens. 

Our Heavenly Father knows them and permits them in our lives, because we have asked in Jesus’ name. Amen
