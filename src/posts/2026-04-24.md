### Verse for Today

> ‘Jesus said to them, ‘I am the bread of life; whoever comes to me shall not hunger, and whoever believes in me shall never thirst’  
> **[John 6:35]**

---

### Reflection

For today and forever, the ‘I am’ points to our true source of rejuvenation and life. The challenges that come our way today may be different from those that stand formidable tomorrow. It may also be that the underlying causes for our suffering remain intact and may seem as invincible as ever. Some of us have even given up hope of ever being able to replenish the preternatural state of our minds (past glory and prowess), because of what seems to be unfolding before us in its intensity today. 

Here, Jesus assures us that whatsoever be it, that which dampens our spirits and zeal, He is our panacea. With Him we conquer all that depresses, that disappoints, that hinders our progress (hunger). And whoever has reposed his trust in Jesus would never ‘fail’ or be forsaken in life (thirst). 

Joshua, who was commissioned (by the Lord) to lead His people to possess the Promised land (Canaan), was never without difficulties. Every inch of the promised land was to be acquired by combat and struggle. But it was the Word, the Lord, who fought each battle and gave them the victory. Even after the land was secured, opposition and attacks from their neighbours persisted. 

The life of Paul, who was called to be the Apostle to the Gentiles, was never the same again. All that he encountered from then on was imprisonment, persecution, insecurity, attacks from the wild, natural calamities…… But he proclaimed that in the midst of all these, Jesus was His peace, and His grace was all that was needed for Him. 

The same applies to us, who claim to be ardent believers, but easily get subdued by the least of debilitating circumstances. Jesus is the bread of life to us, who strengthens, revives, and replenishes our being to fearlessly and confidently take on our life’s journey forward. Amen
