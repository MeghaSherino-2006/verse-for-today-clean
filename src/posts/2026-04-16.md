### Verse for Today

> ‘For the works that the Father has given me to accomplish, the very works that I am doing, bear witness about me that the Father has sent me’  
> **[John 5:36]**

---

### Reflection

The Son of Man did not come to be served, but to serve, and to give His life as a ransom for many. Jesus defined this purpose of His to his disciples, so that they/we could all understand what the works of the Father that had been entrusted to Jesus (for being accomplished) were. We, His disciples, also have a purpose of ours, predestined by the Father, and the only path to faithful accomplishment is: by denying the self, serving, and giving ourselves to many around. 

The state of the world today is pathetic, firstly because a vast majority live merely to exist, without a sense of purpose, a realization of who they are, and what they have been entrusted to do. The second issue that the world today is tormented with is its unpreparedness to take on what it has been called to undertake because of either sloth, unwillingness, or procrastination, inability of the soul to sweat, to bear pain or strive hard to obtain its due – all because of its disconnect with the Father. Even Jesus stated that He did nothing on His own, but only what was shown and told to Him by the Father. Jesus hailed John the Baptiser as the greatest among all men on earth, for he did only what aligned with his purpose in life – to bear witness to Jesus. What was ordained to him by God, what was revealed to him by God’s Spirit (through prayer and austerity) was what helped John attain success and acclamation of the Lord. 

We may not be capable of emulating a lifestyle of austerity that characterised the life of John the Baptiser. But we can reach out to the Lord, asking Him to lead us to our purpose in life. Not just that, but also to be with us all the way that we may do what He pleases and what He desires of us, to make a mark, our signature in life. It is not in looking around, at the crowds, at marvels, cultures, and ways of the world that we learn to be our true selves, but in looking at Jesus and following His commands for us.
