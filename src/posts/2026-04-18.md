### Verse for Today

> ‘…. a complaint by the Hellenists arose against the Hebrews because their widows were being neglected in the daily distribution. And the twelve summoned the full number of the disciples and said, it is not right that we should give up preaching the Word of God to serve at tables…...’  
> **[Acys 6:1-3]**

---

### Reflection

The sequence of events that occurred in the Early Church is described thus: that with great power the apostles were giving their testimony to the resurrected Lord Jesus. Those who believed them were of one heart, mind, and soul – they considered everything they had in common, so there was not a needy person among them (as we read in Chapter 4). Many signs and wonders were done in Jesus name by the apostles, and more and more disciples were added to their community. 

It was then that a complaint arose among them that the Hellenist widows were being ignored in the daily distribution. In response to this, a meeting was summoned of all disciples by the twelve to arrive at a practical turnaround on this issue/allegation. Issues, complaints, and criticisms are usual in any community of people, but how these are handled could make the difference in contributing to the growth of that community or to break/destroy it halfway. Often, what we tend to forget is that divisiveness is caused by the enemy – in any gathering of persons, be it a family, institution, Church, congregation, whatever. To take on the enemy would mean to strengthen one’s resolve to carry on with the cause despite the odds. 

When all odds are placed against us, human strength may fail to withstand and move on. The only way forward then is the spiritual door. It is when the issues, troubles, and confusion are placed before the Lord in prayer, that we would find the rest and peace to carry on despite all heaviness. Many may disagree with taking on this path, thus, how the twelve chose to do it would need to be understood by us. The issue raised was accepted by them and delegated to a group of spirit-filled people (ironically, from the very community ‘who’ felt they were disregarded), while the apostles chose to prayer over them and continue to preach the Word. The wisdom and wit that these unlearned men of the Lord demonstrated was indeed the power and grace of the Holy Spirit in them. They never chose to justify themselves, or deny any allegation made, nor did they underplay the emergence of the issue, but offered it to the Lord with all humility.  Thus, we read that not just did the number of people who followed them increase, but that even the Jewish priests began to accept the faith of the people of the Way – The Early Christians. All that transpires in our lives too is just a step away from being a fruitful witness to the Lord and His Gospel. Only that You and I need to offer them in prayer to Jesus. Amen
