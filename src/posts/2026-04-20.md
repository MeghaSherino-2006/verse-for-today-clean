### Verse for Today

> ‘Let my soul live and praise you, and let your rules help me’ 
> **[Psalm 119:174]**

---

### Reflection

Striving towards one’s purpose in life is this state, when the soul meets with the horizons of joy, serenity, and comfort in God’s Word. It is no longer sorrowful or desperate, and leaves behind its cravings for the things of this world. 

In fact, all the yearning, struggles, and sorrows that came by were those that culminated in its state of purpose. And it was not merely that these contributed to the end automatically, but that there was a desperate clinging of the soul to the precepts of the Lord. In its suffering, agony, resentments, and challenges, the soul was firm in the hands of its Creator, it was faithful, and thus, it withstood all these tests of time and emerged strong to adorn its true goal. St Paul described a similar experience of his in the Letter to the Philippians (Phil 1:12) ‘All that transpired in my life, was for the advancement of the Gospel’. The imprisonments he underwent, the struggles, the afflictions in the sea and on land, attacks of wild animals, persecution from Jews…….all added their own worth to the spread of the message of the Lord. And thus the apostle concluded that I boast more gladly about my weaknesses, for through them grace (power) was made manifest to all. The Spirit of Jesus reminds us of this, to enlighten our minds to seek the Lord at all times. In times of suffering, disgrace, loneliness, despair, trials and tribulations, to seek Jesus and His Word. For by His Word, power is manifested to strengthen our souls to carry every burden for the sake of Christ and His Kingdom. Thus, let us pray with the Psalmist

---

###  Prayer

‘ Let your rules, and judgements lead me, O Lord, let my soul be strengthened to trust and conquer all that is transcient and temporal. It is only then that I lose grip of all that weighs me down to sour higher and seek the eternal. 

Wishing you a fruitful Monday. Amen
