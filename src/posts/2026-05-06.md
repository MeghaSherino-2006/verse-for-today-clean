### Verse for Today

> ‘By this my Father is glorified, that you bear much fruit and so prove to be my disciples’ 
> **[John 15:8]**

---

### Reflection

What does it cost to be favoured by God our father? 

By clinging to Jesus, we become fruitful. We, who are otherwise so overwhelmed by the things of this world, learn to value and prioritise the tenets of the Gospel above every other thing. We tend to thirst for peace, justice, reconciliation, docility, compassion, and mercy. It is then that we slowly begin to inherit the Kingdom of God, while living on the earth. 

It is such as these who are called the salt of the earth, with the capability to season others. You and I, have been called by the Lord to be ‘salt’ for the earth, the light lit by Jesus, not to be hidden under the bushel basket of worldly pride. The world and its desires will come and go, but what remains finally is our heart and its inclinations, by which we will be judged on that day. Bearing fruit need not necessarily mean proof of good deeds, or charity, nor would it mean being able to perform deeds of power – healings, deliverance, and miracles; the fruits that the Lord expects of us are our unwavering trust in Him (faith), the disposition of our hearts that come out as words and attitudes of life (hope), and of true grit in us to persevere every trial/adversity for the sake of love and for propagating life to those around (love). Even the most insignificant of deeds, routines, lifestyles, and vocations in life can prove rewarding, not because they were humungous in outcomes – results, success, or fame (quantity), but because they stood firm (in the midst of all odds) with a resolve to fulfil the will of God and thereby their purpose of life. May all that we do today be demonstrative of our discipleship of Jesus. Amen
