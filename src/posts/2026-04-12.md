### Verse for Today

> ‘Open to me the Gates of righteousness, that I may enter through them and give thanks to the Lord’  
> **[Psalm 118:19]**

---

### Reflection

The Gate is Jesus Christ, our Lord. As each of us is granted this entry, what more can we do but thank the Lord! 

You and I may be withheld, inhibited, and distanced from the hope that emerges from the resurrected Lord because, firstly, we are all sinners, and also because of the imperfections that constitute our time and space today. On this day, Jesus asks us merely to seek His mercy, trust in His overflowing love for us, and reciprocate what we have received to those around. 

A very usual consequence that follows this soul, who completely trusts in the Lord is what the Psalmist tells us in this psalm – The Lord becomes His strength, His song, and His joy. It is the right Hand of the Lord that has done valiantly, that has seen us through the thick and thin of life’s challenges. Much more to go as we navigate ahead, to our Eternal abode, our rightly inheritance, that which Jesus has obtained for us. 

Therefore, with the Apostle St Paul, let us hold fast to the truth, So then, it depends not on human will or exertion, but on God who has mercy. And let us with confidence plead with the Lord as Moses did: Lord if your presence does not go with us, do not send us up from here.

---

###  Prayer

Be with us O Lord, that we may live to recount your wonder works each day in our lives. Wishes to you on this Mercy Sunday. Amen
