### Verse for Today

> ‘He flees because he is a hired hand and cares nothing for the sheep. I am the good shepherd. I know my own and my own knows me’  
> **[John 10:13,14]**

---

### Reflection

Haven’t we often experienced that in life, when a crisis hits hard, it takes much time and effort to regain our calm, let alone the power within. Because until then, it is the pride in us, our ego, our efficiency, our capabilities that been running the show. These can never help build up the resilience and strength the most essential life skills one needs to endure the tests of time. 

These are the hired hands, those we have equipped from the world around while in our comfort zones, those that we have learned and unlearned from people around us, the persons upon whom we have reposed our trust, our confidence, believing that these would be there for us, when our strength fails. To some (among us) before whom wisdom has exposed (earlier in life) hard realities of being rejected, becoming redundant, unusually despised and condemned by these hired hands, our hearts would wait patiently for the one who is our true shepherd. 

The Lord knows His own, and all those whom the Father had given to Him (those who wait patiently for Him). Paradoxically, many fail to realize His unfailing love for them despite the fact of life’s trials and tests - ‘those’ that were meant to bring them closer to Him. These still tend to seek after a hired hand, still relying on their fleeting pleasures, the persons around them, accommodating with their inconveniences, while at the same time, delaying to seek after the Shepherd of their souls – Jesus.

### Prayer

The knowledge and fear of the Lord is true and sure beginning of wisdom. So let us pray for the wisdom that comes from above, that overwhelms our trivial thoughts, minds, and plans, that leads us to where we truly belong to. Lead kindly Light, O Holy Spirit. Amen
