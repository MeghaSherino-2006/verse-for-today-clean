### Verse for Today

> ‘This poor soul cried and was heard by the Lord and was saved from every trouble’   
> **[Psalm 34:6]**

---

### Reflection

Often, we complain of prayers unheard, but we forget to realize that sometimes these were those prayers made from a heart weighed down by the pressures of our world. The pressures of comparison, one that is only self-centred, devoid of mercy, filled with vanity, that which is loathsome to our Creator. 

We were created for something ‘far better’ we hardly realise at times. To be the hands for the Lord, the eyes, ears, heart, and feet for the Lord, to be the herald of his message to many around us (unaware of true freedom in Christ Jesus). The soul who acknowledges this is humble, lowly, depleted of the weight of this world, free to transcend higher and altruistic (merciful)to all. 

What could have groomed this soul to its current state? Of course, it is God’s Spirit and His Word that have pruned the soul, but it could also be the difficulties, hard realities of life, suffering, fear, failures, rejections, despair, disappointments – the imperfections that have cropped up in our life to open to us the threshold of the perfect – the love of Jesus. 

It is then that when this soul cries, there is an answer, an alternate way, there is hope. Not that the troubles suddenly faded as if it were snow in sunshine. But how these (troubles) could be looked at, from the viewpoint of the resurrection of the Lord, from a higher pedestal, from the perspective of sons and daughters of His Kingdom. That we were meant to encounter them, take things by our stride, because He is with us and will be so to the end of the world. That there is something greater that we were called for to obtain while on this earth itself. King David cried so when he saw the deliverance from the Lord, even while face to face with his enemies. We are to acquire this kind of proximity with the Lord, one that will draw the Heavens to us. Amen
