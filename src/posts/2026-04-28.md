### Verse for Today

> ‘Glorious things of you are spoken, O City of God’  
> **[Psalm 87:3]**

---

### Reflection

This City of God is you and me. 

Our paths and trajectories are different, facets in life may seem all the more parallel. But each of us has all the way gone through diverse occurrences, experiences, and encounters in life, that which have defined our thought patterns and life-styles. 

There may have been fault lines, anomalies, and failures in the past. But as we stand today before the Lord, we are on a firm footing, because the Lord says that it was He who established us on holy ground. The elevated plane, the rough paths, the rocky terrain, were all His doing to shape us to an awareness of ‘who’ we really are. And more than all, the Lord declares that ‘glorious’ things are spoken of you and me. He who stood with us mightily as we navigated through all the challenges, delights in us. He desires to see us bear fruit and be glorious. If at all, we have felt hard to believe, it is because we are still stuck in the past, unable to see through the promise of glory that the Lord has declared for us. Let us ask Jesus, to create in us a renewed spirit that cherishes to receive the glories to be unravelled in life.

### Prayer

Lord, increase our faith, that we may see and hear all that You have desired to open for us today. Amen
