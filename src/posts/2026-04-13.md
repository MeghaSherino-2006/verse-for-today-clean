### Verse for Today

> ‘Ask of me, and I will make the nations your heritage, and the ends of the earth your possession’  
> **[Psalm 2:8]**

---

### Reflection

It is God who grants authority and influence. He does so, in accordance/alignment with His definite purpose, plan, and when man respects and reveres Him. 

Reverence for God would mean (in its larger perspective) to respect/comply with the limits, limitations, restrictions, and do’s and don’ts imposed by Him. Wherever man broke those Laws, limits, boundaries, and limitations set by God, sin ruled the roost over his actions. Individual actions then created a cosmos of insecurity, setting a domino of chaos and disorder, impeding every possibility of well-being for humanity. It is thus, this Messianic psalm encourages each one of us to ask God, to seek from Him the desires of our hearts, for what He can do is much beyond what we ‘ever’ did ask for, or ever expect to receive. 

The Messiah Jesus is the Prince of Peace. To a distraught and disturbed world, grappling desperately for peace, the Holy Spirit calls us to seek from the Lord, to repose our trust in His intervention through all negotiations and discussions for a possible turnaround. When this Spirit of the Lord rules, there is freedom. The freedom to grow, to innovate, to collaborate, to crush poverty, inadequacies, and hatred in the hearts of men. 
---

###  Prayer

Lord Jesus, we pray for the authorities, the Power-houses of our day, that they may be inspired to give up vengeance, hatred, and false pride, all these that provoke rebellion, wars, division among nations and peoples. May they gain wisdom to choose measures to promote welfare against destruction, and prosperity against lethality, to be able to tap further on the promises of AI as participatory agents for a better tomorrow. Just imagine our households being embedded with screenless, versatile, and easily accessible multi-modals that support better life standards for people (including the weak, disabled, and aged). The far side has much more to be unravelled by mankind and here’s where our focus and efforts are needed. Thank you Lord for the insight and inspiration. Amen
