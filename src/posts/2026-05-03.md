### Verse for Today

> ‘For a good angel will go with him; his journey will be successful, and he will come back safe and sound’  
> **[Tobit 5:21]**

---

### Reflection

Tobit spoke this to his wife Anna regarding their son Tobias’s journey (for a purpose). These were words of assurance, of comfort, and direction, to reorient the other to trust in the Lord, more than dwell upon human thinking and reasoning. 

One of the best precedents for parents of this era, who believe more in overt protectiveness and instruction than entrusting their children (to the Lord) while permitting them freedom to work out their purpose with prudence and love. It is true and undeniable ‘today’ than ‘ever’, that it is the family and children who are targeted the most by evil and by the influences of the world. To lose track, or become disoriented, disappointed after a failure is what can easily discourage the younger generation, who are less exposed to the hardships, realities, and tribulations of their previous generations. This calls for prudent parenting, which essentially requires the familiarization of children with the Scriptures and living the Word from childhood onwards (in addition to their education and formation). But what proves even more inevitable for this to transform the personality of a child (deep within) is the testimony of principled parents who lived lives of integrity before them. 

A successful parent is this one, who has attempted to live sincerely, has asserted his/her value system in life, and transmitted the same in word and deed to his/her children. Let us introspect, how much successful we have tried to be, in inculcating values in our children, of respecting others, upholding human dignity above all, and honoring God by word, deed, and spirit. Let us ask for forgiveness, and initiate a change for greater miracles to transpire for generations to come. Amen
